Swiss Beverages Category Performance Report -
Q3-2025
Confidential | Prepared by Market Intelligence | 1976-05-01
Executive Summary
The Beverages category in the Swiss FMCG market delivered strong performance in Q3-2025, with value
growth of 5.9% YoY and volume growth of 1.3%. Migros and Coop continue to dominate distribution, while
Denner and Aldi Suisse are gaining share through aggressive private-label strategies.
Total category value reached CHF 766.9M across the 17 monitored retail accounts.

Key Consumer Trends
· Premiumisation in chocolate and specialty coffee continues to outperform mainstream SKUs.
· Sustainability-certified SKUs (Bio, IP-Suisse, Fairtrade) now represent 31% of category value.
· Private label penetration rose to 42%, driven by economic pressure on mid-income households.
· Health-conscious reformulations (reduced sugar/salt) are a growing driver in Beverages and Snacks.
Opportunities for Retail Partners
We identify 3-5 white-space opportunities where our brands are underrepresented relative to category
benchmarks. Priority accounts for Q-next activation: Coop, Denner, Transgourmet. Expected incremental
revenue: CHF 3.3M.
